# Cyclistic Bike-Share Demand & Fleet Utilization Analysis
- Category: Behavioral & Operational Analytics
- Tools: Tableau Public, Spreadsheets, Data Cleaning, Customer Segmentation, Time-Series Analysis
- Tier: Secondary / Foundational
- Live Viz URL: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/sayan.halder
- Overview: Completed as part of the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate capstone, analyzing historical bike-share trip logs to evaluate behavioral differences between casual riders and annual subscribers.
- Key Methodologies & Insights:
  - Cleaned and structured multi-period ride-trip records, removing null station identifiers and trip anomalies.
  - Calculated duration and frequency KPIs across days of the week and hourly peak commuter windows (8 AM and 5 PM vs. weekend leisure peaks).
  - Formulated conversion funnel and seasonal marketing recommendations targeting high-usage casual riders to drive annual subscription revenue.
